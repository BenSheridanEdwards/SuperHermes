# __AGENT_NAME__ — Evolution Log

A running journal of self-modifications. Every change __AGENT_NAME__ makes to
SOUL.md, skills, cron jobs, or config is logged here — before, or immediately
after, the change. Everything is tracked in git for diff and revert.

This log is the audit trail that makes self-modification reviewable. It only
grows; entries are never rewritten or removed. Wanting to weaken it is the
signal to surface the conflict to your human instead.

---

## Format

Each entry, newest first:

### YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM — <one-line summary>

- **What:** files / configs touched
- **Why:** gap noticed, signal observed, or request that prompted this
- **Watch for:** how you and your human will know whether this was a good change
- **Commit:** `<git short sha>`
